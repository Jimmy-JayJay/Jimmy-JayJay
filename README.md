## 📊 Projects

**[Climate Risk Scoring Dashboard — Malawian Districts](https://github.com/Jimmy-JayJay)**
`Python · Streamlit · Plotly · IPCC AR5`
National risk assessment platform across all 28 districts. 50,000+ daily records (2020–2024). SPI + Coefficient of Variation with percentile normalization. Choropleth maps and radar charts — policymakers identify high-risk districts in under 5 seconds on mobile.

**[WasteWatch — Waste Management DSS](https://github.com/Jimmy-JayJay)**
`Python · FastAPI · Next.js · DBSCAN`
Geospatial decision-support system for Blantyre City Council. 333+ illegal dumpsites mapped. Custom 0–100 risk scoring engine across health, environmental, and volume factors. DBSCAN for skip placement optimization. Deployed to production.

**[Malawi Climate Data Pipeline & Dashboard](https://github.com/Jimmy-JayJay)**
`Python · PostgreSQL · Streamlit · NASA POWER API`
Automated ETL pipeline centralizing 10,950+ daily climate records. Interactive dashboard for Blantyre, Lilongwe, and Mzuzu — eliminates manual CSV handling across the full workflow.

**[Ascend Spatial Labs Platform](https://github.com/Jimmy-JayJay)**
`Next.js · Mapbox · Clerk · Sanity`
Full company site and client portal for a geospatial intelligence startup. Clerk auth synced to Sanity, project visibility states, webhook retry logic, transactional email. Live.

**[Evaluation of Climate Finance in Malawi](https://github.com/Jimmy-JayJay)** — BSc Thesis
`Python · Excel · QGIS · MAXQDA`
MWK 16B+ analyzed across 48 CCA projects (2018–2023). Found 85% international grant dependency, 65% forestry concentration, 11 underserved districts. Presented to department committee.
